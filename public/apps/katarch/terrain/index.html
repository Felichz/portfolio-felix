The playing field

The business, its physical pieces, who buys, what already existed, and the number that governs the whole case.

9 min 13 steps

Architecture Katas are competitions where teams of engineers receive a real company’s brief and have a few weeks to design the complete architecture, which they then defend before a jury. In fall 2020 the case was Farmacy Food, and this course reconstructs, step by step, how the winning team, ArchColider, reasoned through it, using the real documents from their repository.

By the end you will be able to

  • Describe the three physical pieces of the business and the three types of user.
  • Separate what the architect receives ready-made from what they have to build.
  • Calculate the system’s real volume and keep it as a yardstick for everything that follows.

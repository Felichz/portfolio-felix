Field guide

The method in four steps, for your next system.

10 min 12 steps

Take away the Farmacy Food case and a method you can apply to any project remains. The winning team ran it in a fixed order, and the order is part of the method. This last chapter folds the ten previous ones into four steps you can take to your next system.

By the end you will be able to

  • Name the four steps of the method in order, and say why the order matters.
  • Point to the moment in the case where each step happened.
  • Turn each step into a question for your own next system.
  • Know where to check every claim: the teams’ public repositories.

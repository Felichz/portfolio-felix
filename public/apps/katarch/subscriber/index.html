A meal’s journey

From the calendar to the fridge: the subscriber cycle as events.

13 min 14 steps

The previous chapter ended with an open question: what about the subscriber, who orders for the whole week? This chapter follows one of their meals from the calendar to the fridge, and back again when they change their mind.

By the end you will be able to

  • Explain why the team kept the subscription as a schedule instead of creating every future order up front.
  • Follow a scheduled meal through its commands and events, from the calendar to the fridge.
  • Explain why an order only becomes available when the fridge confirms it.
  • Read the cancellation of a scheduled order as a two-message refund.

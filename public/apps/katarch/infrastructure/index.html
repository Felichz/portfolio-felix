Landing in the cloud

The design is done on paper; now it has to run somewhere. A private network, identity checked at the gate, and a plan to grow only when the numbers ask for it.

14 min 15 steps

Everything so far is pure logic: modules, messages, boundaries. At some point servers have to be paid for. This chapter follows the modular monolith onto AWS, from the fence around the network to the day a module moves out on its own.

By the end you will be able to

  • Read a VPC: public and private subnets, two availability zones and a single gate.
  • Explain why identity is checked at the load balancer, and why modules still ask each other for credentials.
  • Tell a web of direct calls apart from a log-based stream.
  • Justify scaling up first, and name the signals that trigger scaling out or extracting a module.

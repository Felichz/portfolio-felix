The physical world

Fridges, money and unreliable connections: three real problems, the solution the team designed for each one, and the pattern they share.

16 min 20 steps

This is where the case stops being theory and becomes engineering against reality: two people buying the last meal at the same time, second thoughts with money involved, fridges with no signal.

By the end you will be able to

  • Explain why one actor per fridge makes locks unnecessary.
  • Read a complete purchase in the alphabet of commands and events.
  • Justify event sourcing, acknowledged queues, the 30-second window and the offline PIN as concrete trade-offs.
  • Design for the rainy day too: the path when the hardware fails.

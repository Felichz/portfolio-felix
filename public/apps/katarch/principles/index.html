The rules before the first diagram

First you read the business, then you set tie-breaking criteria, and only then do you design. The order is the method.

11 min 10 steps

The previous chapter left you with one idea: the jury rewarded the quality of the reasoning. This chapter shows how that reasoning is done, in the same order the team did it.

By the end you will be able to

  • Recognize the order of the method: read the business before you draw.
  • Explain the team’s four principles and which constraint each one comes from.
  • Follow the traceability from a business goal to an architectural requirement.
  • Read and write an ADR: context, decision and consequences, including the negative ones.

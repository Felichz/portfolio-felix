What to build and what to rent

A modular monolith only works if the modules are cut well. Where to cut, how to protect what sets the business apart, and what to pay others for.

15 min 12 steps

The previous chapter chose a single application split into modules. This one decides where the boundaries go, and it is the most visual part of the case: five original diagrams, redrawn so you can walk through them.

By the end you will be able to

  • Sort capabilities into core, supporting and generic with two questions.
  • Explain what an anti-corruption layer is for, and follow a piece of data through it.
  • Recognize a facade that buys time without closing doors.
  • Read the metamodel: rules on top, facts below.
  • Give each subsystem its own quality budget by asking what happens if it fails.

Nine chapters of decisions, one at a time. This one sets the ten that truly define the architecture side by side and reads them as a system: what each one solved, what it cost, and which one forced the next.

By the end you will be able to

  • Explain why sixteen ADRs become ten structural decisions.
  • Read every decision as problem, decision and trade-off.
  • Place each decision in its pillar and in the chapter where it was made.
  • Follow the links: which decision forced which, and the chain of trade-offs that runs through the case.
